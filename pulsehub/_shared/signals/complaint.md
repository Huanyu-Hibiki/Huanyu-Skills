# Complaint Signal

The user is unhappy, frustrated, or criticizing a product/service.

## Score Impact

**negative** (mostly) — Usually skip. **But** high intel value for product/market research.

Exceptions where complaint = opportunity:
- Complaint about **competitor** → you can offer your product as alternative
- Complaint about **category pain point** → your product uniquely solves it
- Complaint about **counterfeit / fake** → trust-building opportunity

## Variants by Category

### Generic Complaints

- 太贵了 / 不值 / 性价比低 / 不划算
- 差评 / 翻车 / 踩雷 / 退货
- 售后差 / 客服差 / 物流慢
- 别买 / 别上当 / 避雷 / 千万别买
- 假货 / 高仿 / 山寨 / 仿冒
- 失望 / 后悔 / 想退 / 退款

### Competitor-Specific (Opportunity!)

- "XX 牌子太差了" (named competitor)
- "再也不买 XX 了"
- "XX 售后真的垃圾"
- "刚买的 XX 就坏了"

### Category Pain Points

- "为什么没有 XXX 的功能" (unmet need)
- "都太难用了" (general frustration)
- "等了好久都没有合适的" (supply gap)

### Quality / Reliability

- 用了一周就坏 / 没多久就废
- 掉漆 / 掉色 / 起皮
- 卡顿 / 闪退 / 发热
- 假货多 / 怕买到假的

## Match Rules

- Same matching rules as other signals
- **Critical**: distinguish between complaining about *your* product vs *competitor* product
- Use named-entity recognition (NER) for "XX 牌子" → check if XX is competitor or self

## Suggested Action

### If complaint is about COMPETITOR

**Action**: Engage (carefully)

1. **Empathize** — "理解你的感受，确实..."
2. **Don't trash the competitor** — looks unprofessional, may violate advertising law (中国广告法禁止贬低竞品)
3. **Highlight your differentiator** — "我们在这方面做了 X 优化..."
4. **Soft invitation** — "有兴趣可以试试" (no hard sell)

**Risk**: If the original poster discovers you're a competitor, they may accuse you of "踩同行". Keep tone helpful, not promotional.

### If complaint is about YOUR product

**Action**: Engage (urgently)

1. **Apologize sincerely** — no corporate-speak
2. **Ask for order ID / contact** — move to DM
3. **Promise resolution** — and follow through
4. **Public follow-up** — once resolved, comment again "已解决，感谢反馈"

**Risk**: Mishandled complaints escalate. Always involve customer service team.

### If complaint is general (no brand)

**Action**: Usually skip. Sometimes intel.

- Log as market research data
- Note the pain point for content ideas (write a "如何避坑" post later)
- Do NOT engage as a brand — looks opportunistic

## False Positives

- "差评" used as casual intensifier — "这波操作差评！" (joke, not actual complaint)
- "退货" in shipping context — "支持七天无理由退货" (return policy, not complaint)
- Sarcasm — "真是个宝贝，气死我了" (affectionate, not complaint)

## Cultural Notes

- 中国广告法第十三条：广告不得贬低其他生产经营者的商品或者服务。**Never** name a competitor negatively in public comments — legal risk.
- "翻车" originally meant vehicle accident — internet slang for "things going wrong". Mild complaint.
- "踩雷" originally meant stepping on a landmine — internet slang for "got scammed / bad experience". Stronger complaint.
- "避雷" is the **pre-emptive** version — "let me help you avoid the bad thing". Often used in 测评 content.

## Strategic Notes

Complaints are **gold for content ideas**:

- Track recurring complaints in your niche → write "如何避坑" content
- Track competitor complaints → highlight your differentiator in your own posts
- Track category pain points → product development insights

But for **comment engagement**, be selective. Engaging with every complaint makes you look like a vulture.
