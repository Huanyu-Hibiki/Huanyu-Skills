# Question Intent Signal

The user is asking for advice or information — researching before deciding.

## Score Impact

**medium** — Engage if you have time. Less urgent than purchase intent but builds authority.

## Variants by Category

### Generic

- 求推荐 / 求安利 / 求建议
- 怎么选 / 怎么挑 / 选哪个
- 有推荐吗 / 有什么好的
- 值得买吗 / 值不值 / 划算吗
- 有用过的吗 / 有人试过吗 / 谁买过
- 怎么用 / 怎么做 / 怎么操作
- 哪个好 / 哪个好一点 / 对比
- 新手 / 小白 / 第一次

### Comparison / Decision

- A vs B / A 还是 B
- 对比 / 横评 / 哪个强
- 优缺点 / 优势 / 劣势

### How-to / Tutorial-seeking

- 教教我 / 怎么搞 / 怎么弄
- 步骤 / 流程 / 方法
- 求教程 / 求攻略

## Match Rules

- Same as purchase-intent (case-insensitive, word boundaries)
- Often appears with question mark ? or ？
- Often appears at the **end** of a sentence (Chinese pattern)

## Suggested Comment Angle

When `question-intent` fires:

1. **Give a substantive answer** — not just "用我们的产品"
2. **Frame as helpful expert** — "我做这块 X 年，给你几个建议..."
3. **Provide decision framework** — "看场景：如果是 A 用 X，如果是 B 用 Y"
4. **Mention your product as one of multiple options** — never the only option (looks biased)
5. **End with an open question** — invites follow-up, increases engagement

**Critical**: Questions are your chance to build authority. Don't waste them on lazy "buy my product" replies.

## False Positives

- "求推荐" in a creator's CTA — they're asking audience to recommend *to them*, not asking for themselves
- "怎么用" in a tutorial title — the post *is* the tutorial, not a question
- Rhetorical questions — "难道就没有好用的吗？" (complaint, not seeking)
- "求推荐 + 任意金额" — paid recommendation request (creator solicitation)

## Cultural Notes

- 知乎 questions are the **highest quality** question-intent signal — long, detailed, well-researched. Worth long answers (200+ chars).
- 小红书 questions are short and visual — answer should match (concise + visual).
- B站 comments are casual and meme-heavy — match the tone.
- 抖音 questions often in video — check transcript for the question itself.

## Engagement Strategy by Platform

| Platform | Answer length | Tone | Best timing |
|----------|---------------|------|-------------|
| 小红书 | 50-150 chars | Friendly + emoji ok | < 1 hour |
| 知乎 | 200-500 chars | Authoritative | < 24 hours (long tail) |
| B站 | 100-200 chars | Casual, meme-aware | < 6 hours |
| 抖音 | 50-100 chars | Casual | < 1 hour |
| 公众号 | 100-300 chars | Thoughtful | < 48 hours |
